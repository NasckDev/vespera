Monsters there are 'echoes' of human things. Hunters of the Order of the Lantern go in carrying lanterns. Mood: melancholic but hopeful; cozy warm town versus eerie rifts.

Please create ONE game map background image for this game.

WHAT TO DRAW: The Bell Tower district of Lumeira at dusk, headquarters of the Order of the Lantern: a large stone courtyard (walkable) with a training yard (wooden dummies and racks of practice weapons against the walls). Lantern posts with amber light. Exits centered on the LEFT, RIGHT and BOTTOM edges. The middle of the courtyard is free of objects.
