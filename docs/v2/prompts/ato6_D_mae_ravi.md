# Parte D1 · Ato VI · Mãe do Ravi: retratos

Um prompt por imagem. Respeite a idade descrita (a Mãe do Ravi tem ~45 anos).

### D1-01 · `retrato_mae_ravi_1.png` · Mãe do Ravi (neutral, firm and tired)

**Anexe estas referências antes de mandar:** [estilo de personagens](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NasckDev/vespera/v2/docs/v2/referencias/estilo_npcs_proporcao.png) · [estilo do caçador](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NasckDev/vespera/v2/docs/v2/referencias/cacador_folha_original.png)

```
I am making VÉSPERA, a 2D pixel-art action RPG for PC. It takes place in Lumeira, a lantern-lit coastal town. Twenty years ago the sun did not rise for a whole year. Since then, every dusk, glowing magenta rifts called Breaches open into the Underside, an inverted copy of the town made of everything people lost or threw away. Monsters there are 'echoes' of human things. Hunters of the Order of the Lantern go in carrying lanterns. Mood: melancholic but hopeful; cozy warm town versus eerie rifts.

Please create ONE character portrait for this game: Mãe do Ravi, a washerwoman from the poor Lower Streets, around 45: worn work-reddened hands, a faded headscarf over dark hair with grey strands, a simple patched dress with rolled-up sleeves, a wet apron, firm and tired, dignified; a normal adult woman (1.0x the hunter height).
Expression: neutral, firm and tired.
STYLE: detailed hand-painted pixel art, crisp pixel clusters, dark selective outlines, no blur, no smooth vector look, no 3D render look. Palette: lantern amber #F2A541, rift magenta #E64A8E, deep indigo #1B1F3B shadows, violet #4A2C6B, cream #F4E7C6 highlights, moss green #5C8C4A, earth brown #8A5A3C, stone grey-blue #6B6F8A, water teal #3C7A8A. Light always comes from the TOP-LEFT. Match the attached reference images as closely as possible.

FORMAT: bust portrait (head and shoulders), facing slightly to the right, square image 1024x1024 or larger, flat solid pure green #00FF00 background (it will be removed). Realistic proportions, NOT chibi, NOT big-headed.
Do NOT add: text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, UI, frame or border, grid lines, extra characters.
```
