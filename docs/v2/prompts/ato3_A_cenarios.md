Please create ONE game map background image for this game.

Along the bottom edge: moored fishing boats on teal water and a long stone breakwater wall with many small names carved into it (no readable letters, just scratch marks). Lantern posts with amber light along the quay. Quay exits centered on the LEFT and RIGHT edges. The middle of the quay is free of objects.
