```
I am making VÉSPERA, a 2D pixel-art action RPG for PC. It takes place in Lumeira, a lantern-lit coastal town. Twenty years ago the sun did not rise for a whole year. Since then, every dusk, glowing magenta rifts called Breaches open into the Underside, an inverted copy of the town made of everything people lost or threw away. Monsters there are 'echoes' of human things. Hunters of the Order of the Lantern go in carrying lanterns. Mood: melancholic but hopeful; cozy warm town versus eerie rifts.

Please create ONE game map background image for this game.

WHAT TO DRAW: The Orchard Hill neighborhood of Lumeira at dusk: a gentle hill of apple trees and vegetable gardens with stone-bordered dirt paths (walkable) crossing the image, small wooden fences, beehives and garden beds along the edges, Dona Celeste's herbalist cottage with a small glass greenhouse at the top-center (closed door), one special apple tree next to it that still bears ripe red fruit. Paths exit centered on the left, right and bottom edges. The middle of the paths is free of objects.

STYLE: detailed hand-painted pixel art, crisp pixel clusters, dark selective outlines, no blur, no smooth vector look, no 3D render look. Palette: lantern amber #F2A541, rift magenta #E64A8E, deep indigo #1B1F3B shadows, violet #4A2C6B, cream #F4E7C6 highlights, moss green #5C8C4A, earth brown #8A5A3C, stone grey-blue #6B6F8A, water teal #3C7A8A. Light always comes from the TOP-LEFT. Match the attached reference images as closely as possible.
CAMERA: straight top-down view, everything axis-aligned like the attached plaza: walls, streets and exits run perfectly horizontal or vertical, no diagonal or isometric angle. Walls and objects are seen slightly from the front (3/4 top-down), the floor from above.
FORMAT: one single full image, landscape 16:9, the highest resolution you can produce (at least 1600 px wide). Show only this one map. The walkable floor must be clearly readable and free of characters and creatures. Exits must be wide openings exactly centered on their wall.
Do NOT add: text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, UI, frame or border, grid lines, extra characters.
```
