# [Arte v2] Armas de verdade: 10 armas diferentes por classe (3 imagens)

Hoje os ícones de arma são **a mesma espada (e o mesmo arco e o mesmo martelo) só trocando de cor** em 10 ranks, num estilo simples que não combina com os personagens pintados. Cada rank precisa de uma arma **com desenho próprio**, ligada ao lugar onde ela é forjada na história (ROTEIRO-V2). Um prompt por imagem; anexe as referências indicadas. Cada imagem traz as 10 armas de uma classe numa grade 5×2.

Depois de gerar, mande o arquivo com o nome indicado (`armas_lamina.png`, `armas_arco.png`, `armas_martelo.png`).

### W-01 · `armas_lamina.png` · 10 espadas da Lâmina

**Anexe estas referências antes de mandar:** [identidade do Lâmina](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NasckDev/vespera/v2/docs/v2/referencias/lamina_novo_estilo.png) · [estilo arco (lado)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NasckDev/vespera/v2/docs/v2/referencias/arco_andar_lado.png) · [estilo martelo (lado)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NasckDev/vespera/v2/docs/v2/referencias/martelo_andar_lado.png)

```
I am making VÉSPERA, a 2D pixel-art action RPG for PC set in Lumeira, a lantern-lit coastal town. Twenty years ago the sun did not rise for a whole year ("the Year Without Sun"). Every dusk, glowing magenta rifts called Breaches open into the Underside, an upside-down copy of the town made of everything people lost. Hunters carry brass lanterns and forge weapons from crystal Fragments that monsters drop. Mood: melancholic but hopeful; warm amber lantern light versus eerie magenta rifts.

Please create ONE image: an inventory icon sheet with 10 different SWORDS for the sword hunter shown in the first attached image, arranged in a grid of 5 columns x 2 rows, every cell the same square size (256x256 or larger), each sword centered in its cell, drawn diagonally from bottom-left (hilt) to top-right (tip), all at the same scale. Order (left to right, top row first) and design — every sword must have a clearly DIFFERENT silhouette, not a recolor:
1. Rusty Blade: short, chipped, rust-brown iron, cloth-wrapped grip.
2. Forge Blade: clean dark steel, simple brass guard (made by a stern blacksmith).
3. Orchard Blade: blade with a subtle wood-grain pattern, root-shaped guard, a small purple apple-shaped pommel.
4. Tide Blade: wavy sea-steel with barnacles near the guard, teal glint.
5. Candle Blade: black iron, thin, with a tiny candle-flame engraved near the hilt, wax drips on the guard.
6. Bell Blade: broad bronze blade with a bell-shaped guard and engraved rings.
7. Street Blade: heavy cleaver-like blade forged from scrap, patched with rivets, red rag on the grip.
8. Underside Blade: dark blade cracked with glowing magenta crystal veins.
9. Twin-Dusk Blade: double-edged blade split down the middle into two parallel edges, amber and violet.
10. Dawn Blade: radiant pale-gold blade with a small sun-shaped guard, soft warm glow (legendary, the best one).
ART STYLE IS MANDATORY: the same detailed hand-painted pixel art as the attached hunters (thick dark selective outline, crisp pixel clusters, same shading and palette mood). Light always comes from the TOP-LEFT. Palette: lantern amber #F2A541, rift magenta #E64A8E, deep indigo #1B1F3B, violet #4A2C6B, cream #F4E7C6, moss green #5C8C4A, earth brown #8A5A3C, stone grey-blue #6B6F8A, water teal #3C7A8A.
FORMAT: flat solid pure green #00FF00 background everywhere (it will be removed), no text, no numbers, no frame borders, no shadows on the background.
NEVER: no text or letters, no recolors of the same sword, no blur, no smooth vector or 3D look, no hands holding the swords.
```
