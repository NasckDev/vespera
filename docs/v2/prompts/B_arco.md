```
I am making VÉSPERA, a 2D pixel-art action RPG for PC. It takes place in Lumeira, a lantern-lit coastal town. Twenty years ago the sun did not rise for a whole year. Since then, every dusk, glowing magenta rifts called Breaches open into the Underside, an inverted copy of the town made of everything people lost or threw away. Monsters there are 'echoes' of human things. Hunters of the Order of the Lantern go in carrying lanterns. Mood: melancholic but hopeful; cozy warm town versus eerie rifts.

Please create ONE sprite strip for this game. Subject: hooded hunter with a deep red-violet hood and cape, light leather armor, an old brass lantern on the belt, holding a wooden bow.
Animation: shooting: draws the bow, aims, releases an arrow (the arrow leaves the frame), recoil. Exactly 6 frames. facing DOWN, towards the camera.
The character is shown in the attached walking strips; copy its exact look. Stage 1 = apprentice hunter, simple worn gear.

Light always comes from the TOP-LEFT. Match the attached reference images as closely as possible.

FORMAT: the 6 frames side by side in ONE single horizontal row, every frame the same square size (256x256 or larger), evenly spaced, the subject centered in each frame with the feet on the same baseline. Background: flat solid pure green #00FF00 everywhere (it will be removed), no ground shadow. The SAME character in every frame: same face, clothes, colors, proportions and size; only the pose changes, so the frames play as smooth animation.
Do NOT add: text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, UI, frame or border, grid lines, extra characters.
```
